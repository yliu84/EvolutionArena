## Coral-gecko playable visual slice

`models/coral-gecko-lowpoly-v1.glb` is the cleaned 32k-triangle geometry source.
`models/coral-gecko-rigged-v2.glb` is the accepted locomotion source with a 20-bone
quadruped skeleton and `Idle`, `Run` and `Turn` clips. `models/coral-gecko-rigged-v3.glb`
is the accepted first-version combat-motion source master: it preserves the same 32,000-triangle web LOD,
adds an independently weighted jaw bone, and embeds `Bite`, `Claw`, `TailSwipe`, `Hit` and `Death` alongside
the locomotion clips. `models/coral-gecko-rigged-v4.glb` is the current stage-0 runtime revision. It preserves V3 and the same gameplay/clip contract, but narrows the Jaw influence to the lower snout, limits unsafe inherited Bite/Claw rotations and fixes all exported bone-scale keys at 1.0. The stage-0 runtime uses V4 at a
1.25 scale and crossfades between the three clips. `Run` plays at 3.2x to match
the 5.8 world-unit movement speed; a pooled 14-sprite dust effect emits two
warm, ground-hugging puffs at diagonal foot contacts and fully fades after 0.58 seconds. Meshy 5 generated the source
under CC BY 4.0; Blender cleanup and project-authored rigging retained three 1K
PBR textures and rebuilt normals/tangents. Meshy attribution is required in
public game credits.

The accepted first-version runtime character master adds a non-authoritative weight layer over
the same GLB: eased visual acceleration/deceleration, step compression, stop
settle, head/tail turn inertia, contact-shadow response and two-foot dynamic root
grounding. Material tuning strengthens the existing normal map and roughness
response without adding downloads. The source currently has no AO-mapped
material; this limitation is exposed in debug data rather than hidden.

The player uses only `Space` for the primary chain: Bite, Claw and TailSwipe cycle in order, with one buffered input accepted during the active/recovery animation. All three are normal-attack animation variants, not skills; the separate skill-attack system is intentionally disabled and deferred. `H`, `K` and `R` remain QA-only reaction/reset controls. These clips do not define damage. The accepted combat profile is `coral-gecko-combat-master-v1`. Its contact shadow uses three correctly oriented ellipses instead of the earlier circle, so the body, head and tail produce a readable grounded footprint without the terrain clipping it into a semicircle.

The Quality 3D demo also contains a procedural armored training creature. It is a disposable validation target rather than a production monster asset: normal attacks own its damage/contact rules, while flash, pooled fragments, small camera trauma, knockback and the world-space health bar are presentation only. It cannot attack and automatically respawns in front of the player after death.

Each normal-attack step now reacquires the one live demo target and rotates the character root toward it at up to 12 radians/second before contact; a hit is refused above 8 degrees of residual aim error. The embedded animation revision uses a larger jaw opening and forward lunge for Bite, higher alternating foreclaw arcs and torso twist for Claw, and a wider four-joint coil/sweep for TailSwipe. These changes affect direction readability and silhouette only, not skill state or damage authority.

On 2026-08-15 the user accepted this complete package as the first mother-monster production reference. New monsters may use different rigs and attacks, but their asset budget, grounding, named-state coverage, targeting, hit feedback, validation evidence and documentation must be measured against this master.

## Scarlet-gecko first evolution

`models/scarlet-gecko-rigged-v1.glb` is preserved as the historical first-evolution master derived from the coral-gecko web master. The user later rejected its visible result because the stretched body read as flat and rubber-like and its material/color response no longer met the current target. It is no longer loaded at runtime.

`models/scarlet-gecko-meshy-source-v2.glb` and `models/scarlet-gecko-meshy-walking-source-v2.glb` preserve the user-generated textured and rigged Meshy inputs. `models/scarlet-gecko-rigged-v2.glb` is the active replacement candidate: 19,406 visible character triangles, 27 bones and nine named clips. It uses an independent full-volume quadruped anatomy, coral/teal/cream embedded textures and a semi-matte zero-metal response. The exported invisible 80-triangle Blender bone-shape helper is explicitly hidden by every runtime loader.

Stage 1 uses the user-accepted baseline `scarlet-gecko-first-evolution-master-v2`, combat profile `scarlet-gecko-combat-master-v1` and display scale 1.661. Its revised Run uses 1.45 playback, a 1.22 safe authored-limb delta amplification and 5.8 presentation foot contacts/second. Runtime Run/Walk clones retain full pitch and limb motion but reduce the imported Hips/chest/head axial sway to 0.22 yaw and 0.18 roll, preventing the 11–16° source sway from making sustained W appear diagonally aimed. Idle/Run entry also stops every other animation action, so a clamped attack, Hit or Death final pose cannot remain blended after combat or reset. The source GLB's full-strength base-color emissive reuse is limited to 0.18 shadow fill; 1.16 contrast, 1.24 saturation, 0.46–0.64 roughness and 0.50 environment response restore lit body planes while preserving coral, teal and cream texture detail. Keyboard/touch motion is camera-relative with a signed screen-right projection, and living player/prey bodies reserve collision spacing during travel, chase and attack approach. The shared stage-0/1 chain is `Bite → Pounce → TailSwipe`; stage-1 Pounce keeps horizontal travel scale 0.32 but uses independent vertical lift scale 1.65 so the complete body visibly leaves the ground. Damage authority, selected-target rules, 8-degree contact tolerance and disabled skills are unchanged. Source, hashes, processing and validation evidence are recorded in `../../../docs/concepts/evolution-v2/scarlet-gecko/`.

`models/scarlet-hunter-rigged-v2.glb` is the replacement candidate. It keeps one coherent 32,000-triangle skinned mesh, 21 bones and eight required clips, while rebuilding the silhouette around a broad chest, heavy pelvis, compact length, thick tail root and smaller crown-to-body ratio. Its recalculated skin weights follow the changed anatomy. A burnished crimson/copper material treatment preserves scale breakup, pale surfaces, normals and roughness instead of using a crushed-black floor. No rigid attachments or new downloaded textures are used.

`models/scarlet-hunter-rigged-v3.glb` is the accepted stage-2 runtime master. Its test baseline is `scarlet-hunter-native-toon-candidate-v1`; the realistic master and the derived 7,999-triangle candidate remain preserved for comparison.

`models/scarlet-hunter-native-toon-source-v2.glb` and `models/scarlet-hunter-native-toon-rigged-v2.glb` replace V1 at runtime after the user found the fully faceted body too coarse.
