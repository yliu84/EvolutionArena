## Three self-checks before generating the other three views

1. **Would a player want to be this?** If it looks diseased, skeletal, insect or
   creepy, it failed — that is board-1.
2. **Two large cyan eyes?** They must be the focal point and read from far away.
   Tiny slit eyes fail.
3. **Four or five LARGE pods at the neck and shoulders?** Big and few. A long
   row of small identical spine bumps is board-1 again. Legs must be sturdy
   muscular columns, never sticks.

Do not reuse the board-1 checks (thin blade, broken line of light along the
spine, mostly black). Passing those is what produced the rejected look.

## Meshy run — two things that went wrong last time

Both cost a round trip on this exact form. Do not repeat them.

1. **Export type must be `Animation → Walking (with skin)`.** The first job used
   `Character output`, which returns Meshy's generic auto-rig: 68 joints named
   `Bone_000…Bone_067` and **zero animation clips**. The registry rig mapping,
   `requiredNodes` and every processing script depend on the 27-bone quadruped
   template (`Hips`, `chest`, `head`, `tail1..3`, `frontleg`, …), and with no
   Walk clip there is no source gait to derive Walk and Run from.
2. **Upload the front view as an extra view, not the 3/4 alone.** The second job
   returned geometry identical to the first because only the 3/4 was given; the
   generator had nothing to pin the width against.

## Two cautions specific to this board

Neither is a rejection — decide with your eyes when the mesh lands.

- **The tail tuft is a soft glowing wisp in the concept.** Image-to-3D will make
  it solid geometry. A solid stylised flame shape is fine and normal in game art;
  a cyan blob is not. Check it on the side view.
- **The head horns are thin.** Thin protrusions reconstruct unevenly. They are
  decoration, not identity — if they come back mangled, that alone is not worth
  a re-run, but check they have not fused into the skull.

## What the mesh must still hold

The appeal gate (`../PRODUCTION-MODEL-CONTRACT-V1.md` §0) is judged first and by
eye. After it passes, these are arithmetic:

- `l/h ≤ 2.10` — measure with
  `node scripts/measure-glb-proportions.mjs --height 2.55 --max-lh 2.10 <file>.glb`
- emissive area ≤ 15% of the texture
- 27 bones, a Walk clip present
- **The torso-width gate is withdrawn.** It is what pushed the previous board
  thin and skeletal; see the contract's §3 second correction.
