# Goal 7 — Build Expression and External Playtest Readiness

## Decision

Goal 6B proved that the River Valley can finish; Goal 6C made encounter tier
readable. Goal 7 now tests the game's selling point: a player must feel that
hunting different prey created a different playable build, not merely a
different-looking creature.

## Scope

1. Audit the existing mutation catalogue against three visible facts: the
   player can identify its trigger, feel its benefit and understand its cost or
   tactical limit.
2. Complete the presentation and result-language gaps that prevent Genes from
   explaining weighted mutation candidates. Genes remain weights, never a
   promised drop table.
3. Validate one tactical answer per Fang, Shell and Swarm direction against
   common prey, an Elite and a Boss. This is a rules-and-feedback pass; it does
   not add skills, new player models, maps or map-environment assets.
4. Record genuine 18–22 minute sessions: first mutation, selected mutations,
   deaths, victory/defeat, player-reported confusion, visible P1/P2 faults and
   mobile FPS.
5. Run at least three external, no-explanation sessions using the normal River
   Valley entry. Fix observed P1/P2 issues only; do not dilute the test by
   expanding content during it.

## Acceptance

- A tester can explain what prey Genes did to their candidate choices.
- Two different builds make a visibly different decision against at least one
  Elite or Boss.
- The result screen accurately reflects the run's Genes and selected
  mutations.
- There are no P0/P1 issues in the three external sessions.
- A real landscape mobile device either sustains 30 FPS or has a recorded,
  reproducible performance bottleneck with a scoped follow-up.

## Out of scope

- New skills, mana, cooldown slots or multiplayer.
- New map environment assets, shrine, lighting or vegetation.
- Batch production of creatures or a redesign of accepted player models.
